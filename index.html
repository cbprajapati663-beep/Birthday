A small note

For my Miss Perfect

Waqt ne hum dono ko shayad alag mod par la khada kiya hai. Kabhi baat hoti hai, kabhi baaton mein anban bhi… magar kuch ehsaas shor nahi karte, bas khamoshi se dil mein apni jagah banaye rakhte hain.

“Kuch raaste adhure rehkar bhi yaadgaar hote hain,
kuch log door hokar bhi dil ke paas hote hain.
Main har mod par tumhe awaaz nahi deta,
bas ek umeed rakhta hoon — kabhi fursat mile,
toh hamari baat phir kisi muskurahat se shuru ho.”

“Tumhari hansi se roshan rahe har subah tumhari,
khushiyon se mehakti rahe har shaam tumhari.
Jo khwaab dekho, woh haqeeqat ban kar milein,
aur zindagi har mod par le aaye muskurahat tumhari.”

Main aaj bhi intezaar ke us mod par hoon jahan umeed abhi zinda hai. Bas itni si khwahish hai ki yeh intezaar itna lamba na ho jaaye ki milne ya baat karne ka mauka hi waqt ke haathon guzar jaaye. Phir bhi, tumhari marzi aur tumhara sukoon mere liye zaroori hai. Koi shikayat nahi — sirf ek sachcha ehsaas.
